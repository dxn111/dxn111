## Selected projects

A few projects that represent the directions I'm exploring.

### [Sonderr](https://github.com/dxn111/sonderr)
An AI-focused software project exploring a more capable, developer-oriented workspace for working with models, tools, and workflows.

### [Sonderr Swarm](https://sonderr-swarm.vercel.app)
An experimental multi-agent environment focused on coordinating specialized agents across complex tasks and software workflows.

### [Causal Packet Networks](https://github.com/dxn111/causal-packet-networks)
An experimental architecture concept exploring alternative ways to structure information flow in AI systems, with an emphasis on efficiency. It is an ongoing research direction, not a claim of proven performance.

### [HHR-AI](https://hhr-ai.vercel.app)
An early-stage experiment investigating predictive token generation and whether anticipating likely next tokens can help reduce perceived response latency.

### [Dockside-OS](https://github.com/dxn111/Dockside-OS)
An experimental operating-system project exploring a console-like, controller-first experience for mobile hardware.

> These projects are works in progress. I share prototypes and research honestly, including limitations and unfinished ideas.

## Principles

- **Build, don't just speculate.** Turn ideas into prototypes that can be tested.
- **Be honest about results.** Separate hypotheses, experiments, and verified outcomes.
- **Learn in public.** Share knowledge and make useful work accessible.
- **Think long-term.** Some ideas need years of iteration, not a weekend demo.
- **Stay curious.** Good engineering comes from asking better questions.
